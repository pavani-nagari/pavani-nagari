### 🐬 Hey there, welcome to my little corner of the ocean!

I'm Pavani, a software engineer with **6+ years of full-stack experience** building enterprise applications across frontend, backend, APIs, databases, and cloud. I'm excited by the challenge of bringing AI into real products—connecting LLMs, retrieval, and agentic workflows to the services and interfaces people already use.

I earned an **M.S. in Computer Science and Engineering, specializing in AI/ML, from the University at Buffalo (3.87 GPA)**. I'm exploring GenAI, RAG, and tool-using agents, while keeping the engineering essentials close: evaluation, reliability, and thoughtful system design.
